HexClear Privacy Policy

Updated: 26 August 2026 · App ID: com.hexclear.app

Your scores, settings, and saved games live on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to a HexClear server. Uninstall the app and that data goes with it.

Advertising

Ads come from Google AdMob. Google may use an advertising ID and basic device info to serve and measure ads. In places like the EU and UK, we ask before that happens. You can change this later in Android’s ad settings, or in HexClear > Settings > Ad settings when that option is shown.

Google’s own policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Accounts and Data Collection

HexClear does not make you an account. It does not ask for your name, email, or location. There is no chat and no cloud save.

Children

This game is not meant for children under 13.
